* 1. Instant Audience Hook (____ / 2 Points)
* 2 pts: Explicit, short target (e.g., "Over 50?", "At 55:", "Type-2 Diabetics:").
   * 1 pt: Passive, buried target (e.g., "...if you are over 50").
   * 0 pts: Generic or missing entirely.
* 2. The High-Value Health Promise (____ / 2 Points)
* 2 pts: Punchy, high-stakes physical outcome (e.g., "Lower Blood Sugar", "Flush Arterial Plaque").
   * 1 pt: Vague health tip (e.g., "Feel better", "Stay healthy").
   * 0 pts: No specific promise or benefit.
* 3. The "Without" Pain-Killer (____ / 2 Points)
* 2 pts: Instantly eliminates a massive dietary restriction, cost, or frustration.
   * 1 pt: Mentions a weak or highly generic objection.
   * 0 pts: Forces a sacrifice without giving relief.
* 4. Sharp Data / Timelines (____ / 2 Points)
* 2 pts: Uses exact numbers, days, or ingredient counts (e.g., "14 Days", "3-Food").
   * 1 pt: Uses soft modifiers (e.g., "fast", "soon", "easy").
   * 0 pts: Completely abstract and unmeasurable.
* 5. The 3-Second Simplicity Test (____ / 2 Points)
* 2 pts: Under 18 words, zero academic jargon, high-impact readability.
   * 1 pt: Slightly wordy or relies on confusing scientific terms.
   * 0 pts: Dense, long, or reads like a textbook title.
